ABOUT THE PROTOTYPE

A safer campus, by design

A student concept exploring privacy-conscious coordination—not an official University of Ibadan service.

i

This is a research prototype. It is not connected to the University of Ibadan, campus security staff, emergency services, cameras, or mobile devices.

01

What you can explore

Navigate the dashboard, browse fictional incident reports, view a schematic zone map, and inspect sample charts. You can submit a sample report to see how the interface responds.

02

Privacy by default

No personal data is requested or stored. Sample reports use broad invented zones, with no identities, exact coordinates, device identifiers, or live sensor feeds.

03

Edge computing concept

The research concept proposes processing sensitive signals close to where they are generated and sharing only minimal, aggregated information when appropriate. This website does not implement edge processing or ingest sensor data.

04

Before any real-world use

Deployment would require university authorization, community consultation, a privacy and impact assessment, security testing, accessible reporting alternatives, and clear human-led response policies.
